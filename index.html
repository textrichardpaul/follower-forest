    Mission

    Every follower becomes part of a living forest.
    One person, one tree — growing with its community.

    Vision

    A vast, thriving forest of thousands of named trees.
    Seasons and wildlife, grown entirely by its followers.

    How it works

    • Follow, and a tree is planted with your username on it.
    • Trees grow and bear fruit as forest days pass.
    • A forest day passes about every four minutes, a season in about eight hours, and a full year in one real day. The forest keeps its own time and grows while you are away.
    • Wildlife arrives as the forest grows.
    • Use Find your tree to see yours.
    • Each reel, the most-liked comment plants the next organic addition.
